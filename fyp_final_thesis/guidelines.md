## 1. Introduction and Purpose

* Many students experience uncertainty regarding the expected content of each chapter of a research thesis, even after completing substantial project work.


* This document clarifies the structure and content expectations for the Abstract and all major chapters from Introduction through Conclusion.


* These guidelines complement the official University of Moratuwa (UoM) thesis template.


* They provide structured direction for preparing the Interim Progress Review Report and the Final Thesis for CM 4900 - Research Project in Artificial Intelligence.


* The guidelines emphasize systematic scientific writing and are designed to support incremental thesis development.


* Students first prepare an Interim Progress Review Report and later extend it into a complete Thesis by adding evaluation and concluding components.



---

## 2. Using the Guidelines

* After reading this Guideline document, you are required to follow the Progress Report/Thesis template, which is developed based on the UOM Template mentioned above.



### 2.1 Interim Progress Review Report

* The Interim Progress Review Report must follow the thesis template from the Cover Page up to and including the Implementation chapter.



### 2.2 Final Thesis

* The final thesis is produced by extending the Progress Review Report and adding the Evaluation chapter as well as the Conclusion and Further Work chapter.


* Students are expected to refine earlier chapters during the final submission.



### 2.3 Practice Tool

* Students are encouraged to use the interactive academic writing platform: [https://inpraweb.lib.uom.lk/](https://inpraweb.lib.uom.lk/).


* InPRAWeb provides a gamified environment to practice writing proposals, reports, theses, papers, and presentations.

## 3. Chapter Structure and Contents

* The thesis follows a Milestone Approach (MA): Abstract → Literature Review → Technology → Approach → Analysis & Design → Implementation → Evaluation → Conclusion.



### General Writing Rules

* Every chapter must include an Introduction and a Summary.


* All figures and tables must have numbers, titles, and captions.


* Figures and tables must be cited in the text.


* Maintain logical continuity between chapters.


* Avoid repetition across chapters.


* Use formal academic language.


* Use consistent terminology throughout.



---

## 4. Abstract

* The abstract should be approximately one page.


* It must include a brief introduction to the research area.


* It must include a description of the research gap (≈ 1½ page).


* It must outline your solution (≈ 3½ page), including hypothesis, inputs, process, outputs, theoretical extension, positioning the extension within AI body of knowledge, evaluation, and conclusion.


* The abstract must emphasize your contribution.


* Do not include citations.


* Do not include abbreviations.


* Do not include references.


* Do not include future work.



---

## 5. Introduction

* Suggested subsections include Introduction, Objectives, Problem in Brief, Background and Motivation, Novel Approach to , Resource Requirements, Structure of the Thesis, and Summary.


* The chapter must clearly include the objective to conduct a critical literature review.


* It must include the objective to perform an in-depth study of relevant technology and define research gaps.


* It must include the objective to develop an extension to bridge the research gap.


* It must include the objective to evaluate the extension's contribution to AI knowledge.


* Additional objectives may be included if justified.


* This chapter must contain strong academic citations.



---

## 6. Literature Review

* The chapter title should be meaningful (e.g., Developments and Challenges in Autonomous Drone Systems).


* The Literature Review must connect directly with the Introduction.


* It must organize prior work chronologically or thematically.


* It must discuss strengths and weaknesses of existing approaches.


* It must include a comparison table of prior studies.


* It must identify technological research gaps.


* It must clearly define the research gap.


* This chapter must contain extensive scholarly citations.


* End the chapter by summarizing findings and introducing the next chapter.



---

## 7. Technology

* Rename this chapter to reflect the technologies studied (e.g., Research Challenges in Transformer Architectures).


* This chapter provides a comprehensive study of the technology you intend to extend.


* Include a justification for selecting the technology.


* Position the technology within the AI body of knowledge.


* Discuss the theoretical foundations of the technology to be extended.


* Detail the strengths and limitations of the technology.


* Include a discussion on the research gap connected with Chapter 2.


* Include revisiting the research gap.


* If multiple technologies are used, organize them under major subsections.


* Include an introduction, citations, and a summary.



---

## 8. Approach

* Rename the chapter to reflect your proposed solution (e.g., A Novel Approach to Extending Transformer Architecture).


* This is the core conceptual chapter.


* It must introduce and name your extension.


* It must describe the hypothesis, inputs, outputs, processes, users, features, and technologies.


* The process description must logically demonstrate how the technological gap is addressed.


* This chapter serves as the conceptual blueprint of the project.



---

## 9. Analysis and Design

* This chapter converts the conceptual solution into a system architecture.


* It must include the top-level architecture bridging the research gap.


* It must include the logical positioning of the extended contribution.


* It must include the rationale for architectural design.


* It must include key extension concepts.


* Do not include algorithms or implementation details in this chapter.



---

## 10. Implementation

* This chapter explains how the architecture is realized.


* For each module, include the software tools used.


* For each module, include hardware platforms.


* For each module, include algorithms.


* For each module, include flowcharts.


* For each module, include pseudocode.


* For each module, include brief code snippets.


* Extended technical material and screenshots should be placed in appendices.



---

## 11. Evaluation

* This chapter demonstrates whether objectives were achieved and is required for the Thesis only.


* Include the experimental setup.


* Include datasets or test cases.


* Include participants (if applicable).


* Include performance metrics.


* Include results in the form of tables, graphs, or charts.


* Evaluation must align directly with the Approach chapter.


* Raw data and questionnaires may be included in appendices.



---

## 12. Conclusion and Further Work

* This chapter must quantitatively summarize achievements.


* It must state the achievement of each objective.


* It must discuss limitations.


* It must describe encountered problems.


* It must suggest realistic future work.


* Future work must be concise and relevant.



---

## 13. References

* All in-text citations must appear in the reference list.


* IEEE referencing style is mandatory.


* Use a reference manager (e.g., Zotero).


* Each reference must include author(s), year, title, source, and page numbers where applicable.



---

## 14. Appendices

* Appendices may include SRS documents.


* Appendices may include a project charter.


* Appendices may include schedules.


* Appendices may include a glossary.


* Appendices may include a user guide.


* Appendices may include extended implementation details.


* Appendices may include questionnaires.


* Appendices must support the thesis without overwhelming the main content.



---

## 15. Submission Procedure

* Submit the report/thesis to the Department of Computational Mathematics for examination.


* Revise the document according to the examiner's feedback.


* Obtain supervisor approval.


* Submit the corrected version to the department.


* After receiving supervisor consent, upload the thesis PDF to Moodle.


* After receiving supervisor consent, upload the zipped source code.
